Completion at a glance

Who filled in each section?

Complete means all home context answers selected for that period, or both school ratings, are present. Optional comments and names are not required. Missing means an empty section on a past scheduled day; today and future dates are shown separately.

Participation and regulation

Counts and percentages use the available ratings for each checkpoint, not all possible days. Missing ratings are never treated as settled or successful participation.

Weekly regulation pattern

Weeks start on Monday. Changes in who completes the tracker and which checkpoints are recorded can change the apparent pattern.

Morning context and school observations

These are co-occurring observations, not proof of cause. Each denominator includes only days with that morning answer and at least one school rating. “Difficulty observed” means any recorded regulation difficulty or non-participation; it does not describe the whole day.

Supports tried

Reported effects describe the support combination used at that checkpoint. They cannot establish the effect of one strategy on its own. “Not sure” and missing effects are excluded from the known-effect denominator.
