Parent practice · short and sustainable

This section is for coaching and preparing materials. Do not share the clinician authenticated view with parents.

5 minutes, 3–4 days a week, using only the clinician-approved **training** words. Not the cold-probe list. Model clearly, invite one or two attempts, respond positively and stop while the activity is enjoyable.
Repeatable home routine
1. Listen (30 seconds)Parent names each card. Child can point without producing it.
2. Model (1 minute)Parent gives a clear word model; child attempts with support.
3. Play (3 minutes)Feed the characters, post letters or race toys while practising approved cards.
4. Celebrate and finish (30 seconds)Record cards attempted; don't re-test the word list at home.
