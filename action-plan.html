05 · WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Alex brings the information together

Once the team has contributed, Alex will review and consolidate the information and suggestions.

Alex will identify common ground, differences in perspective and any information still needed, then bring a proposed next step back to the team for discussion and confirmation.

Changes to supports, new resources, referrals, assessments or monitoring will arise from that process. Suggestions here are not yet agreed actions or commitments.

Where tracker information is available, Alex will collate it and bring it into the review.
