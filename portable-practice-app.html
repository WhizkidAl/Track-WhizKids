Speak instead of typing

Tap a text box so the Samsung keyboard opens. Tap its microphone (in the toolbar or near the bottom, depending on the tablet). Speak a short sentence. Tap Stop or the keyboard icon, check the words, then save. If it is missing: Samsung Keyboard settings → Voice input → choose Samsung voice input or Google voice typing if available. Permission and internet requirements vary.

Microphone recordings play your voice. Keyboard voice input turns speech into written words. Word and sound recordings are made in the online Speech room and link to the selected item there. They stay in that browser’s online-tool storage; they are separate from this START file’s welcome-book recordings and backup.
