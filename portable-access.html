Working together

How to use this plan

Add what you know, correct what is unclear, and leave questions where the team has not agreed yet. The goal is not to make every box perfect; it is to create one shared picture that can guide practical decisions.

Family

Add what daily life looks like, what matters most, what helps, and what feels unrealistic or burdensome.

School

Add what is happening in the classroom, what supports are actually being used, where implementation is difficult, and what appears to help participation.

Clinicians

Add assessment findings, clinical reasoning and recommendations. Keep observation, interpretation and decisions clearly distinguished.

Everyone

Use the final sections to decide what will be standardised, what needs to be built, who will do it and what remains unresolved.

Shared content

Current picture and next decisions

The text below is the shared working document. Existing clinical draft content may already be present; edit it together.

Purpose of this plan

What are we trying to improve by coordinating supports?

Strengths, interests and successful participation

What engages the participant? When do things go well? What conditions make participation easier?

Communication access

What helps the participant understand, express needs, ask for help, make choices and communicate when regulation is harder?

Regulation and sensory supports

What helps regulation? What can increase arousal? What should adults notice or offer early?

Current supports already in place

List what exists now across home, school and therapy. Include resources that are available but not yet being used consistently.

What appears to be working

Include successful tools, environments, interactions or routines. Do not only document problems.

Current barriers

Where is participation difficult? Where are supports unclear, inconsistent, impractical or unavailable?

Family priorities

What would the family most like the team to understand, change or build?

School priorities

What does the school need to make support workable in the classroom and across the day?

Consistency: visuals

Which visual supports should be the agreed core set? When and how should each be used?

Consistency: language

What common wording, key phrases or signs should adults use with the agreed visuals?

Consistency: regulation and self-advocacy

How should break, quiet, movement, choice, help and co-regulation supports be offered?

One-page About Me / support passport

Would a concise portable summary help new adults understand communication, regulation, strengths and support needs?

Portable physical support folder

Would a small folder of the agreed core supports help keep implementation consistent across adults and settings?

Parent-owned shared workspace / calendar

Would a shared parent-controlled space improve access to current resources, meeting decisions and provider information?

Assessment and coordination

What assessment is underway or still needed? What information needs to be shared to avoid duplication and conflicting recommendations?

2027 transition / handover

What needs to be portable into the next setting or team? What should be established before transition?

Agreed actions

Write concrete actions with a responsible person and timeframe where possible.

Unresolved questions

What still needs discussion, observation or assessment before a decision can be made?

Document status

Where is the team up to?

This does not lock the plan. It simply tells the team whether it is still being built, ready for a coordinated review, or reflects the current agreed approach.
